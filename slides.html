Opening slide. This is the working prototype, not a template.

It answers four questions, instantly

What are we posting?

A calendar of every post, with the caption and script attached.

What needs to be created?

A clear list of what still has to be filmed, shot or written.

Who are we waiting on?

Every job has a name and a due date on it. No chasing.

Is our content working?

Real numbers, plus plain-English advice on what to do next.

Everything else in the app exists to answer one of these four.

If Brian remembers nothing else, it should be these four.

The Dashboard

Open it and see what matters: this month's numbers, and exactly who owes what.

Works the same on a phone — the action list is the first thing you see.

The Content Planner

A calendar of everything going out, with the script and caption inside each item.

Drag a post to a different day to reschedule it. On a phone it becomes a simple list.

Create with AI

Describe what you want and it writes the post or makes the graphic, already on brand.

It uses DELCO's real logo, colors and mascot — no design work needed.

Content Requests

The shared to-do list between the marketing team and DELCO.

Technicians get a text with a link and upload from the job. No app, no login.

The Content Library

Every photo, video and graphic in one searchable place, tagged and labeled.

Nothing gets lost in someone's camera roll. Pick an asset and turn it into a post.

Analytics

How posts actually performed, and what to do more of next week.

Numbers are demo data for now. Live Instagram and TikTok stats get wired in later.

Brand Hub and LUNA

Where DELCO teaches the software its brand — and an assistant that knows all of it.

Set the brand once and every future post follows it automatically.

Every post leaves a trail

Nobody has to ask where something stands or who dropped it. The software keeps the record, so the conversation stays about the work.
